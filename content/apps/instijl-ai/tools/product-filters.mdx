The **Product Filters** tool uses AI to automatically assign the correct filters to your products. Based on the existing product information and the filters already present in your online store, the AI determines which filters best fit a product.

> **Please note:** This tool only assigns existing filters and does not create new filters itself.

## How does assigning filters work?

Open the tool via **Products** and click **'Filters'** next to the desired product.

A screen will now open showing **Possible filters**: an overview of all filters currently available in your online store. Below that, you configure the **Assignment behavior**:

- **Add selected assignments:** The filters suggested by AI are added to the product's already existing filters.
- **Replace assignments within selection:** The filters suggested by AI replace the existing assignments within the shown selection.

You can also provide **Custom rules (optional)** to give the AI further instructions.

Next, click **'Generate assignments'**. The AI tool analyzes the product and suggests the most suitable filters. Review the proposal and confirm to assign the filters to the product.

## Assigning filters in bulk

Do you want to assign filters for a large number of products at once? Use the bulk function for this.

> **Please note:** Bulk functionality depends on your subscription type. You need at least a **Growth** plan.

Use the search function on the products page to select the desired products and start the bulk task. Just like with the other AI tools, a test is first run on 1 product, so you can check the settings before the full batch is processed.

## What can I do with the Product Filters tool?

This tool specializes in:

- Automatically recognizing and assigning the most suitable, existing filters to a product.
- Choosing between adding filters or replacing assignments within the selection.
- Taking your custom rules into account while assigning.
- Assigning filters in bulk for your entire assortment.
- **And much more!**
