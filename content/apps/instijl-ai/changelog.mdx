### 31-08-2026

- **Release:** Automatically fill Related Products with AI by using the **Automations** tool. 🎉
- **Improvement:** Renamed "Batch tools" to "Bulk tools".
- **Improvement:** Many improvements to the UI, platform and AI processing.

### 27-07-2026

- **Release:** [Workflow automations](https://www.instijl.support/nl/apps/instijl-ai/workflow-automation) are now in public beta! 🎉
- **Improvement:** Many UI bugfixes and improvements.

### 08-07-2026

- **Release:** The [Prompt Library](https://www.instijl.support/apps/instijl-ai/prompt-library) is now released for all users! 🎉
- **Improvement**: Users without a subscription can now test the app with 25 free credits.

### 30-06-2026

- **Release:** Accessibility Audit tool is now available on Growth and Pro subscriptions
- **Bugfix:** Fixed several issues

### 29-06-2026

- **Release:** [InStijl.AI](http://InStijl.AI) is now available in the [Lightspeed App Store](https://services.webshopapp.com/login/?redirect=/store/apps/1625/) 🎉
- **Release:** [InStijl.AI](http://InStijl.AI) Toolbar for Lightspeed is now available in the [Firefox Extension store](https://addons.mozilla.org/en-US/firefox/addon/instijl-ai-lightspeed-toolbar/) 🎉

### 22-06-2026

- **Feature:** Better email-notifications informing you of your subscription status.
- **Bugfix:** Fixed the category-filter for products
- **Bugfix:** Fixed a problem with webshops profiles that were being overwritten.

### 03-06-2026

- **Release:** [InStijl.AI](http://InStijl.AI) is now offically out of beta
- **Improvement**: Many improvements and bugfixes

### 20-05-2026 (beta)

- **Feature**: You can now edit texts before approving the AI-written texts.

### 18-05-2026 (beta)

- **Bug**: Improved context for the Blog Writer.

### 13-05-2026 (beta)

- **Release**: The beta-program starts today.
